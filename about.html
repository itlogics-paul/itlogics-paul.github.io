Who We Are

Rockwood-Based. Ontario-Focused. Long-Term Oriented.

ITlogic Solutions has been a trusted technology partner for Ontario municipalities and small businesses for over 20 years. We're based in Rockwood, Ontario, and we serve organizations across the province — from small township offices managing aging infrastructure to growing businesses navigating their first serious security compliance requirement.

Our background is deep in municipal IT — building networks from the ground up, managing infrastructure through multiple technology generations, and supporting the applications and systems that public-sector organizations depend on every day. That experience shapes how we approach everything we do.

We started expanding into cybersecurity advisory and compliance work because we kept seeing the same problem: organizations doing their best with limited resources, carrying security risk they didn't fully understand, and lacking the documentation to demonstrate due diligence when it mattered most. We built our services to address that gap directly.

Municipal Focus

Why Municipalities Trust ITlogic

Municipal IT is different. Budgets come from taxpayers and require council approval. IT staff are often generalists wearing multiple hats. Systems must remain available around the clock. And public accountability means every decision — especially security decisions — needs to be documented and defensible.

We've built our practice around this reality. Every assessment, policy, and advisory service we deliver is designed to produce the documentation and evidence your organization needs to demonstrate due diligence — to council, to auditors, to insurers, and to residents.
